🔒 Your Privacy Matters: AttendMe is designed with privacy at its core. All your data stays on your device. We don't collect, transmit, or store any of your personal information.

This Privacy Policy explains how AttendMe ("we", "our", or "the app") handles your information. By using AttendMe, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. Information We Don't Collect

AttendMe is built to respect your privacy completely. We do not collect, transmit, or store:

  • Personal identification information (name, email, phone number)
  • Attendance records or work data
  • Salary information or financial data
  • Device information or usage analytics
  • Location data or GPS coordinates
  • Any other personal or sensitive information

2. Local Data Storage

All data you enter into AttendMe is stored locally on your device using secure local storage mechanisms. This includes:

  • Your attendance records (present, absent, half-day, overtime)
  • Salary rate settings
  • Calendar events and notes
  • App preferences and settings

This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us or any third parties.

3. No Internet Connection Required

AttendMe is designed to work completely offline. The app does not require an internet connection to function, which means:

  • No data is transmitted over the internet
  • No external servers are contacted
  • No tracking or analytics are performed
  • Your data remains completely private and secure

4. Data Backup and Export

You have full control over your data:

  • You can export your attendance data at any time
  • Backing up your data is your responsibility (via device backups)
  • If you uninstall the app, all local data will be deleted
  • We cannot recover your data if it is lost or deleted

5. Third-Party Services

AttendMe does not integrate with any third-party services, analytics platforms, or advertising networks. There are no third-party SDKs that collect or transmit data.

6. Permissions

The app may request the following device permissions:

  • Storage: To save your attendance data locally on your device
  • Notifications: To send you reminders about marking attendance (optional)

These permissions are used solely for the app's functionality and not for data collection.

7. Children's Privacy

AttendMe does not knowingly collect any information from children under the age of 13. Since we don't collect any data at all, the app can be safely used by users of all ages.

8. Data Security

While we don't collect or transmit data, we still prioritize security:

  • All data is stored using secure local storage mechanisms
  • Data is only accessible to the app on your device
  • No data is transmitted over networks
  • You are responsible for keeping your device secure

9. Your Rights and Control

You have complete control over your data:

  • Access: All your data is accessible within the app
  • Modification: You can edit or delete any records at any time
  • Export: You can export your data in standard formats
  • Deletion: Uninstalling the app removes all local data

10. Changes to Data Practices

If we ever decide to collect any data in future versions of the app, we will:

  • Update this Privacy Policy with clear explanations
  • Notify users through the app or app store updates
  • Obtain explicit consent before collecting any information
  • Provide opt-out options for any data collection

11. App Store Data

When you download AttendMe from app stores (Google Play Store, Apple App Store), those platforms may collect information according to their own privacy policies. This is independent of AttendMe and governed by their respective terms.

12. Crash Reports and Diagnostics

Currently, AttendMe does not collect crash reports or diagnostic data. If this changes in the future, it will be clearly disclosed and you will have the option to opt in or out.

13. International Users

Since all data stays on your device, there are no international data transfer concerns. Your data never leaves your device regardless of your location.

14. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted in the app and on our website with an updated "Last Updated" date. Your continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

16. Transparency Commitment

We are committed to transparency about our data practices. If you have any concerns or questions about privacy, we encourage you to reach out to us directly.

✨ Bottom Line: Your attendance data is yours and yours alone. It lives on your device, and we never see it. That's our promise.
